## 2. Page Copy (draft)

### Short description (search/listing preview)
> A cozy fantasy café game. Serve warm cups to cold travelers at the crossroads of three worlds. No timers, no fail states — just tomorrow's opening. 🍵

### Long description
> You've inherited your Aunt Marigold's tiny café in Hollowbrook Crossing, where three roads meet between the human town, the elven woods, and the mountain holds. Everyone passing through ends up at your counter sooner or later.
>
> **Brew** drinks with a whisper of magic in them — moonleaf tea, ember cocoa, cloud foam.
> **Meet** six regulars with stories that unfold across your days: a tired courier, a wandering trader, a blacksmith who hasn't opened the door chime in years…
> **Grow** the café your way — a second kettle, a window bench for the cat, music that plays while the rain does its thing outside.
>
> ☕ One in-game day fits a coffee break — progress saves automatically
> 📖 A journal that remembers every recipe and regular so you never have to
> 🐈 A cat named Mops who judges your murky experiments
> 🌧️ Relaxed Mode on by default: no fail states, no rush. Ever.
>
> *Made with care. Runs in your browser.*

## 5. Devlog Strategy (the actual marketing plan)

Cadence: **one post per milestone**, not per week — quality over frequency. Each post: what changed, one GIF, one honest note about process.

1. **Post 0 (pre-M1):** concept art + pillars. "I'm making a cozy café game where you can't lose."
2. **Post 1 (after M1):** core loop GIF + design-pillar talk (P1 Calm over challenge).
3. **Post 2 (after M2):** meet the cast — one illustrated character card per arc beat revealed.
4. **Post 3 (after M3):** content-complete announcement with screenshots.
5. **Post 4 (launch):** the game, the thank-yous, what's next.

Cross-post to appropriate communities following their self-promo rules; keep a simple open-dev notes file so devlog writing takes an hour, not an evening.

## 6. Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] Handle claimed; working-title availability checked (trademark/name search)
- [ ] Game tested logged-out / incognito (fresh-save experience)
- [ ] Save survives: refresh mid-service, tab close, browser restart
- [ ] Audio starts only after interaction (no console warnings)
- [ ] Reduced-motion + keyboard-only passes done this build
- [ ] Credits screen lists all asset licenses (fonts, CC audio if any)
- [ ] Privacy note: "No analytics, no accounts, saves stay in your browser." (decide analytics y/n — default no)
- [ ] Itch page proofread by someone else
- [ ] Fresh install → first drink served < 90 s (final gate, doc 06 §6)

## 7. Post-Launch Rhythm

- Reply to every comment within reason — cozy audiences reward presence.
- Patch notes as devlogs, however small ("Mops now sits in the window bench").
- Watch two numbers only: **plays → day-5 reach %** and **comment sentiment re: stress**. Everything else is noise at this scale.
- When 1.0 ships (seasons + all arcs), flip page to "Released," consider $3–5 price with a free browser demo retained forever.

## 8. Launch Week Runbook

Day 0: publish page + Post 4 devlog together. Pin a comment inviting feedback.
Day 1: reply to all comments; fix any crash-level bugs as hotfix (itch allows instant re-upload).
Day 3: small "thank you" post with a fun stat (cups served globally — trackable locally via sum of serves? no telemetry — instead share YOUR favorite screenshot).
Day 7: retrospective devlog draft; decide next milestone date publicly.
